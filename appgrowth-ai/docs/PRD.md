# Product requirements — AppGrowth AI

**Owner:** Nous Healthcare Solutions · **Users:** app publishers (owners, marketers, analysts), agencies, the platform's Super Admin.

## Problem
Small and mid-size app publishers want predictable, genuine install growth across Android and iOS, but running research,
ASO, creatives and four ad platforms — within a fixed budget — needs specialist time they don't have.

## Goals
1. Add an app and get a realistic, evidence-backed growth plan within minutes.
2. Run app install campaigns on Google, Apple, Meta and TikTok through official APIs, from one place.
3. Never exceed the customer's spending limits; every automated change is explainable and auditable.
4. Report installs honestly: first installs vs reinstalls, organic vs paid, iOS aggregate attribution kept separate.

## Non-goals
Guaranteed install counts; incentivised or fake installs; bypassing platform policies or reviews; holding customer ad
spend (ad platforms bill customers' own accounts; the subscription is billed separately).

## Users and roles
Owner, Admin, Marketer, Analyst, Viewer per organization; Super Admin and Support for the platform (MFA mandatory).

## Key flows
Register → add app (store link) → onboarding (research, ASO, strategy) → approve strategy → connect ad accounts → set
budget → draft campaigns + creatives → approve → launch → monitoring, optimisation within autonomy level → daily/monthly
reports. Monthly targets default to 10,000 installs/app and are configurable; forecasts state what the budget can achieve.

## Autonomy levels
1 Recommendation (people apply changes) · 2 Approved automation (cost-reducing changes automatic, increases need approval)
· 3 Autonomous (anything within hard limits). Emergency stop and per-org/campaign/account switches at every level.

## Plans
Free Trial (14 days, 1 app), Starter, Professional, Agency, Enterprise — prices and limits configurable by the Super Admin.

## Success metrics
Time to first plan < 10 min; % of campaigns within target CPI; zero overspend incidents; monthly target attainment vs forecast.
