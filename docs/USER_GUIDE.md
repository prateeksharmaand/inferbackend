# CareerPilot AI: How to Use It

CareerPilot finds, scores and prepares job applications for you every day at [jobs.inferapp.online](https://jobs.inferapp.online). You review, decide and apply: it never applies or sends a message without your click.

Plan on about 20 minutes once for setup, then 15–30 minutes a day.

## Part 1: One-time setup

- [ ] **Step 1: Sign in.** Open [jobs.inferapp.online](https://jobs.inferapp.online) and sign in with prateeksharmaand@gmail.com. Sign-up is closed, so only you can get in.
- [ ] **Step 2: Check your profile.** Go to Resume Studio → **Profile & skills**.
    - Fix anything wrong in the profile built from your resume: employers, dates, titles, years of experience.
    - Fill in **Current CTC** and **Notice period**.
    - In the yellow "Please confirm" box, tick ✓ each skill you have real hands-on experience with (iOS, CI/CD, Codemagic, Xcode, SSL Pinning and others), and delete the rest. Only confirmed skills are used in matching and in your resumes.
    - Click **Save profile**.
- [ ] **Step 3: Generate your 4 resume versions.** In Resume Studio → **Resumes**, click **Master**, **Flutter Technical Lead**, **Mobile Architect** and **Engineering Leadership**. Download one PDF and check it reads well.
- [ ] **Step 4: Check your search settings.** In **Agent Automation**, automation should show **On**. Preferences are already set: 9 role keywords, 10 cities, minimum ₹25 LPA. Adjust if needed and click **Save preferences**.

## Part 2: Daily routine

Every morning at 10:00 IST the best new jobs arrive by email and on Telegram (@InferJobsBot), already scored against your profile.

1. **Review the shortlist** (**AI Shortlist** page).
    - Jobs are ranked by score: 85+ Excellent, 70–84 Strong, 55–69 Potential.
    - Click a job to see why it scored that way: matching and missing skills, reasons to apply or not, and salary fit.
    - **Shortlist** the ones you like; click **✕** to dismiss the rest so they stop appearing.
2. **Apply** (**Applications** page → open the job).
    - For 85+ matches, the tailored resume and cover letter are already prepared ("Awaiting Approval"). For others, click **Prepare resume + cover letter**.
    - Click **Review & edit** to read the tailored resume. It only reorders and rewords your real experience; anything it refused to change is listed in the report at the top.
    - Click **Approve package**, then **Employer site**, and apply there with the downloaded PDF.
    - Come back and click **I've applied**.
3. **Contact recruiters** (company page → **Find recruiters & hiring managers**).
    - Contacts are saved only with a public source link, never guessed.
    - On a contact, click **Draft**, pick LinkedIn connect or DM, edit it and click **Approve**.
    - **Copy** it, send it yourself on LinkedIn, then click **I sent it myself**.
    - When they reply, click **Log reply**.

Aim for 3–5 quality applications a week.

## Part 3: As things progress

- **Follow up.** At 18:00 IST each day you get reminders for applications with no reply after 7 days. Open the application, click **Follow-up email**, edit it, approve and send.
- **Interviews.** When one is set, open the application, click **Schedule** and enter the date and type. The status updates itself and the interview shows in **Calendar**. Before it, start a mock interview in **Interview Coach** (for example Architecture and Leadership), answer each question and read the feedback.
- **Offers.** Set the status to **Offer Received** and enter the offered CTC.
- **Weekly review.** Each Sunday, read the weekly report and check **Analytics** for which sources give the best matches and your interview conversion rate.

## Finding more jobs and the daily schedule

- **Add a job you found yourself:** Discover Jobs → **Add job**, then paste the URL. For LinkedIn and Naukri, which need sign-in, paste the description instead.
- **Ask in plain words:** in **Career Agent**, try "Show Flutter lead jobs above 30 LPA" or "Which applications need follow-up?"
- **Add companies to watch:** Settings → Job sources → add a company's careers board.

## Things to know

- **Search limit:** the free JSearch plan allows 200 searches a month, so CareerPilot runs about 6 a day and rotates through your role keywords. Company boards and pasted URLs fill the gaps.
- **You stay in control:** CareerPilot never applies or sends anything on its own. Every application and message goes through your click.
- **Notifications:** choose what goes to email and Telegram in Settings → Notifications.
