Privacy Policy

Infer PHR (iOS and Android app) · Last updated: 24 September 2026

1. Who we are

Infer PHR ("Infer", "we", "us") is a personal health record app that helps you keep your medical records, vitals and health documents in one place. This policy explains what information we collect through the app, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. Questions? Email support@inferapp.online.

3. How we use your information

  • To provide the app's features: storing records, charting vitals, reminders and your health timeline.
  • To provide AI features (health chat, drug checker, risk scores, symptom self-check, report analysis) — only if you turn on AI Health Insights.
  • To keep your account secure and to respond to your support requests.

We do not sell your data. We do not use your health data for advertising or marketing, and we do not use Apple Health data for any purpose other than showing it to you in the app.

4. AI features and Google Gemini

Infer's AI features are powered by Google's Gemini API. When you allow AI Health Insights, the data needed for the feature you use — your question, relevant vitals and profile details, or the text of a document you upload — is sent to Google to generate a response. We ask for your permission before any data is sent, and you can switch this off anytime in Profile → AI Health Insights. If you don't allow it, the rest of the app keeps working.

Documents: every time you upload a document, or ask to re-analyse one, the app asks whether that specific document may be sent for AI analysis. If you choose "Upload without AI", the document is saved to your records and is not sent to Google. Reports imported from Gmail are analysed only if you have turned on AI Health Insights.

AI output is for general information only. It is not a medical diagnosis or medical advice — always consult a qualified doctor.

5. Who we share information with

  • Service providers that host and run the app on our behalf: cloud hosting and database providers, Google Firebase (push notifications), Google Gmail API (only if you connect Gmail) and Google Gemini (AI features, only with your permission). They may use your data only to provide their service to us.
  • Legal requirements — if required by law, court order or a government authority.

We never share Apple Health data with third parties.

6. How we protect your information

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Passwords are stored as one-way hashes, login tokens are kept in your device's secure storage, and uploaded documents are served only to your signed-in account.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active. When you delete your account, your profile, vitals, documents and other health data are permanently deleted from our systems.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Access and correct your information in the app's Profile screen.
  • Delete your account anytime in Profile → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account and health data.
  • Disconnect Gmail from the Records screen in the app, or revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Withdraw consent for AI features in Profile, for Apple Health in the iOS Health app, and for notifications in iOS Settings.
  • Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable laws, you may also request access, correction or erasure, or raise a grievance, by emailing support@inferapp.online.

9. Children

Infer PHR is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we'll update the date above and let you know in the app.

11. Contact

Email support@inferapp.online for privacy questions, requests or grievances.
